# Privacy Policy

**Updated: May 31, 2026**

**Effective: May 31, 2026**

Welcome to Cherry Studio (the “Software,” “we,” or “us”). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we process and protect your personal information and data. Please read and understand this Policy carefully before using the Software.

### 1. Information we collect

To ensure normal operation, improve the user experience, and enhance product quality, we collect the following completely anonymous, non-personal operating information in tiers (collectively, “Anonymous Basic Operating Information”). All information is anonymized, contains no data that identifies you personally, and cannot be associated with your personal information. You always retain full control over all information collection, as explained below.

#### (1) Basic operating information

This information is collected by default to ensure basic functionality, security, and version compatibility:

1. Software version information: used for version-compatibility checks, security-update delivery, and similar purposes;
2. Aggregated feature-usage information: overall summaries of feature use, without details of specific usage behavior;

#### (2) Product-improvement information

To improve the product experience continuously, we collect the following information by default:

1. Feature activity and frequency: how actively and frequently features are used;
2. Error logs and crash information: anonymous error logs and crash stacks generated while the Software is running, used for troubleshooting and stability improvements.

This information consists of highly aggregated anonymous data and does not include chat content, API Keys, or any personally identifying information.

How to disable collection: You can disable both categories at any time with “Send anonymous error reports and usage statistics” under “Settings → General Settings → Privacy and Advanced.” Disabling them does not affect the Software’s core functionality.

#### Special note regarding this Policy update

Because this Policy update changes the data-collection architecture, the related toggles under “Settings → General Settings → Privacy and Advanced” will be reset to enabled by default when the new version starts. If you want to retain your previous disabled setting, return to this page after upgrading and change it again. We apologize for the inconvenience.

#### (3) Our commitments

For both Basic Operating Information and Product-Improvement Information, we commit that:

* All data is anonymized and cannot be associated with your personal identity;
* It will not be used for user profiling or advertising, or sold to third parties;
* It will not include your chat content, API Keys, Knowledge Base content, or any other sensitive data (see Section 2).

### 2. Information we do not collect

To protect your privacy to the greatest extent possible, we expressly commit that we will not collect, retain, transmit, or process:

* Your API Keys: model-service API Key information that you enter into the Software;

* Your chat data: any chat data produced while using the Software, including but not limited to chat content, instruction information, Knowledge Base information, vector data, and other custom content;

* Sensitive personal identifying information: any sensitive information that directly identifies you.

### 3. Local storage and data interactions

* Local data: Your API Keys, chat history, personal preferences, and similar data are stored entirely on your local device. Cherry Studio does not provide a cloud synchronization service and does not upload this data to our servers.

* Direct requests: The Software acts only as a local tool that calls third-party model-service APIs. Your device connects directly to servers of the third-party model providers you configure, such as OpenAI and Anthropic. You must not use the Software to create, copy, publish, or distribute illegal or harmful information that violates the laws and regulations of the People’s Republic of China or the country where you are located; harms national security, honor, or interests; undermines ethnic unity; spreads rumors; disrupts social order; promotes obscenity, pornography, or violence; or infringes the lawful rights of others.

### 4. Privacy policies of third-party model providers

Because the Software uses API Keys that you obtain and configure for third-party model providers, when you use AI chat functionality you are receiving service directly from that third-party model provider:

* You are responsible for the privacy and compliance risks involved in using third-party model providers.

* Each model provider processes data you submit, such as chat content, according to its own privacy policy and data-security measures, and may independently collect other data within the scope of its service. This processing and collection is not controlled by the Software, and the toggles under “Settings → General Settings → Privacy and Advanced” cannot disable it.

* To understand or disable such processing and collection, review the privacy policy on the selected model provider’s official website and ask that provider for available opt-out methods. We assume no responsibility for this processing or collection.

### 5. Special notice about third-party services

To help you manage and process data, the Software supports selected third-party services under “Settings → Data Settings.” You choose whether to configure and use them by entering an API address, user name, password, and similar information:

* Cloud backup: WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, and Nutstore.

* Third-party connections: Notion, Yuque, Joplin, Obsidian, and SiYuan.

When you use these third-party services through the Software:

* API keys, passwords, and other credentials you enter are used only locally on your device. The Software does not collect or store any information or data. You are responsible for protecting your credentials.

* Each third-party service is operated independently by its provider. Its data processing—including collection, use, storage, and sharing—is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy and terms. Before connecting, carefully read and understand the provider’s privacy policy and terms of service, use the third-party service in compliance with them, and accept all risks and responsibilities associated with that service. We assume no responsibility for data exposure, loss, noncompliant processing, or similar events resulting from your use of a third-party service.

* These providers may independently collect your data within the scope of their services. That collection is not controlled by the Software, and the toggles under “Settings → General Settings → Privacy and Advanced” cannot disable it. To disable such collection, contact the corresponding third-party provider.

### 6. Policy updates and amendments

This Policy may be adjusted when the Software is updated. When a material change occurs—including but not limited to changes in the scope of information collection, user rights, or processing methods—we will notify you in advance through a prominent notice in the Software, release notes, or similar means, and clearly identify the updated and effective dates in the new version. Please review such changes promptly. If you continue using the Software after the Policy takes effect, you will be deemed to have acknowledged and accepted the updated Policy.

### 7. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Policy or Cherry Studio’s privacy safeguards, contact [support@cherry-ai.com](mailto:support@cherry-ai.com).

Thank you for choosing and trusting Cherry Studio. We will continue to provide a safe and reliable product experience.
