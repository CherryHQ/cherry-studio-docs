# Business Inquiries

Cherry Studio business email:

**[bd@cherry-ai.com](mailto:bd@cherry-ai.com)**

Use this address for Enterprise Edition inquiries, procurement, commercial licensing, and partnership discussions. It is not the preferred support channel for everyday Community Edition questions.

## When to contact the business team

### Cherry Studio Enterprise Edition

The Enterprise Edition is intended for teams that need centralized management of AI resources, organization members, and knowledge assets. The directions currently announced include:

* Unified access to cloud and private local models.
* Shared team Knowledge Bases.
* Employee and permission management.
* Private deployment.
* Enterprise-grade data backup and recovery.

Visit [Cherry Studio Enterprise Edition](https://enterprise.cherry-ai.com) for public information, then use the business email to ask about a demonstration, pricing, and applicable scope.

{% hint style="info" %}
Specific features, deployment methods, service scope, pricing, and delivery times are determined by the proposal and contract that the business team prepares for your actual requirements.
{% endhint %}

### Commercial licensing

Cherry Studio Community Edition uses the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**. Commercial use is not categorically prohibited, but it must comply with the license terms.

If your organization needs a commercial license that is not subject to some AGPL-3.0 requirements, contact the business team. See [Cherry Studio Open Source License](questions/cherrystudio-xu-ke-xie-yi.md) for a basic explanation.

### Ecosystem and joint partnerships

You can send a short proposal for:

* Model-service and API integration.
* Enterprise solutions and private deployment delivery.
* Hardware, operating-system, or distribution partnerships.
* Joint education, research, or community programs.
* Authorized brand, media, or marketing partnerships.

Sending an email does not establish a partnership. Obtain written confirmation before using product names, Logos, promotional claims, or customer cases.

## What to include in your email

To reduce follow-up questions, include the following in your first email:

1. **Organization**: company or institution name, website, region, and contact.
2. **Partnership type**: Enterprise Edition, commercial licensing, model integration, distribution, or another partnership.
3. **Scale**: estimated users, devices, or deployment scope.
4. **Deployment environment**: public cloud, private cloud, local network, or completely offline.
5. **Core requirements**: business problems that must be solved and essential features.
6. **Security requirements**: data residency, authentication, permissions, audit, and compliance.
7. **Timeline**: desired evaluation, test, or launch period.
8. **Contact details**: a work email and time zone for follow-up.

### Example subject lines

```text
[Enterprise inquiry] Example Company / 200 users / Private deployment
[Commercial license] Integrating Cherry Studio Community Edition into Example Product
[Ecosystem partnership] Example model provider API integration
```

### Email template

```text
Organization:

Contact and title:
Partnership type:

Use case:

```

Do not send a production API Key, password, private key, real user data, or an unredacted internal document in your first email.
